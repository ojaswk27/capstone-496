# Fixed-Wing Aircraft Aerodynamics and Design
## Reference Document for Aerospace Design Assistant

### 1. Fundamental Aerodynamic Forces

#### 1.1 Lift Force

The lift equation:
L = 0.5 * ρ * V² * S * C_L

Where:
- L = Lift force (N)
- ρ = Air density (kg/m³)
- V = True airspeed (m/s)
- S = Wing planform area (m²)
- C_L = Lift coefficient (dimensionless)

Dynamic pressure:
q = 0.5 * ρ * V²

Lift can also be expressed as:
L = q * S * C_L

#### 1.2 Drag Force

Total drag equation:
D = 0.5 * ρ * V² * S * C_D

Drag coefficient breakdown:
C_D = C_D0 + C_Di

Where:
- C_D0 = Zero-lift (parasite) drag coefficient
- C_Di = Induced drag coefficient

Induced drag coefficient:
C_Di = C_L² / (π * e * AR)

Where:
- e = Oswald efficiency factor (0.7-0.85 typical)
- AR = Aspect ratio = b² / S

#### 1.3 Lift-to-Drag Ratio

The lift-to-drag ratio:
L/D = C_L / C_D

Maximum L/D occurs when:
C_L_opt = √(π * e * AR * C_D0)

(L/D)_max = 0.5 * √(π * e * AR / C_D0)

### 2. Wing Design Parameters

#### 2.1 Aspect Ratio

Aspect ratio definition:
AR = b² / S = b / c̄

Where:
- b = Wingspan (m)
- S = Wing area (m²)
- c̄ = Mean aerodynamic chord (m)

Typical values:
- Gliders: AR = 20-40
- General aviation: AR = 6-10
- Fighter jets: AR = 2-4
- Delta wings: AR = 1-3

#### 2.2 Wing Loading

Wing loading:
W/S = Weight / Wing_area (N/m² or kg/m²)

Typical values:
- Ultralight: 25-50 kg/m²
- General aviation: 80-150 kg/m²
- Commercial jets: 500-700 kg/m²

Wing loading affects:
- Stall speed: V_stall ∝ √(W/S)
- Gust response: Rougher ride at higher W/S
- Turn radius: Smaller at lower W/S

#### 2.3 Taper Ratio

Taper ratio:
λ = c_tip / c_root

Optimal taper for elliptical lift distribution:
λ ≈ 0.4 for unswept wings

Mean aerodynamic chord:
c̄ = (2/3) * c_root * (1 + λ + λ²) / (1 + λ)

#### 2.4 Sweep Angle

Sweep benefits:
- Delays drag rise at transonic speeds
- Reduces gust sensitivity

Critical Mach number increase:
M_crit_swept = M_crit_unswept / cos(Λ)

Effective dynamic pressure:
q_eff = q * cos²(Λ)

### 3. Performance Calculations

#### 3.1 Stall Speed

Stall speed in level flight:
V_stall = √(2 * W / (ρ * S * C_L_max))

With flaps (C_L_max increases):
V_stall_flaps = V_stall_clean * √(C_L_max_clean / C_L_max_flaps)

#### 3.2 Maximum Speed

Maximum speed (thrust = drag):
V_max = √(2 * T_max / (ρ * S * C_D0))

For propeller aircraft:
V_max ≈ √(2 * P_max * η_prop / (ρ * S * C_D0))

#### 3.3 Rate of Climb

Rate of climb:
ROC = (P_available - P_required) / W
ROC = (T * V - D * V) / W
ROC = V * (T - D) / W

Maximum rate of climb occurs at:
V_y = √(2 * W / (ρ * S)) * √(k / (3 * C_D0))

Where k = 1 / (π * e * AR)

#### 3.4 Service Ceiling

At service ceiling, ROC = 100 ft/min (0.5 m/s):
P_available = P_required + W * 0.5

Ceiling limited by:
- Engine power lapse with altitude
- Reduced air density affecting both thrust and lift

### 4. Range and Endurance

#### 4.1 Breguet Range Equation (Propeller)

Range = (η_prop / SFC) * (L/D) * ln(W_initial / W_final)

Where:
- η_prop = Propeller efficiency (0.7-0.85)
- SFC = Specific fuel consumption (kg/W/s)
- L/D = Lift-to-drag ratio
- W = Weight (initial and final)

For maximum range:
- Fly at (L/D)_max
- Fly at altitude for best SFC

#### 4.2 Breguet Range Equation (Jet)

Range = (V / TSFC) * (L/D) * ln(W_initial / W_final)

Where TSFC is thrust-specific fuel consumption (kg/N/s).

For jets, range is maximized at:
V_MR = V_MD * 3^(1/4) ≈ 1.316 * V_MD

#### 4.3 Endurance

Maximum endurance (propeller):
E = (η_prop / SFC) * (C_L^1.5 / C_D) * √(2 * ρ * S) * (1/√W_f - 1/√W_i)

Endurance is maximized when:
C_L = √(3 * C_D0 * π * e * AR)

### 5. Stability and Control

#### 5.1 Static Longitudinal Stability

For stability, dCm/dCL < 0 (negative slope)

Static margin:
SM = (x_np - x_cg) / c̄

Where:
- x_np = Neutral point location
- x_cg = Center of gravity location
- c̄ = Mean aerodynamic chord

Recommended static margin:
- Stable aircraft: SM = 5-15%
- Aerobatic: SM = 0-10%
- Unstable (fly-by-wire): SM = -5 to 0%

#### 5.2 Neutral Point Location

Approximate neutral point:
x_np/c̄ = x_ac_wing + η * (S_h / S) * (l_h / c̄) * (1 - dε/dα)

Where:
- x_ac_wing = Wing aerodynamic center (~0.25c)
- η = Tail efficiency
- S_h = Horizontal tail area
- l_h = Tail moment arm
- dε/dα = Downwash gradient

#### 5.3 Control Surface Sizing

Elevator effectiveness:
C_m_δe = -η * (S_h / S) * (l_h / c̄) * C_L_α_h * τ_e

Where τ_e is the elevator effectiveness (0.4-0.6 typical).

Aileron effectiveness:
C_l_δa = (2 * C_L_α * τ_a / (S * b)) * ∫[y1 to y2] c * y dy

### 6. Typical Aircraft Parameters

#### 6.1 Cessna 172 (General Aviation)
- Wingspan: 11.0 m
- Wing area: 16.2 m²
- Aspect ratio: 7.5
- Empty weight: 767 kg
- Max takeoff weight: 1,111 kg
- Wing loading: 68.6 kg/m²
- C_L_max (flaps): 2.1
- Stall speed (flaps): 46 kt
- Cruise speed: 122 kt
- Range: 1,185 km
- L/D_max: ~10

#### 6.2 Small UAV (Fixed-Wing)
- Wingspan: 2.5 m
- Wing area: 0.5 m²
- Aspect ratio: 12.5
- MTOW: 10 kg
- Wing loading: 20 kg/m²
- Stall speed: 12 m/s
- Cruise speed: 25 m/s
- Endurance: 2-4 hours
- L/D_max: ~15

### 7. Design Process Overview

1. Requirements Definition
   - Mission profile
   - Payload requirements
   - Range/endurance
   - Speed requirements

2. Initial Sizing
   - Weight estimation
   - Wing loading selection
   - Power/thrust loading

3. Wing Design
   - Aspect ratio
   - Taper ratio
   - Airfoil selection
   - Sweep (if needed)

4. Fuselage Design
   - Internal volume
   - Drag minimization
   - Structural considerations

5. Empennage Design
   - Tail volume coefficients
   - Control surface sizing

6. Performance Verification
   - Takeoff/landing
   - Climb performance
   - Range/endurance check
