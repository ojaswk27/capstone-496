# Aircraft Structural Design and Analysis
## Reference Document for Aerospace Design Assistant

### 1. Structural Design Philosophy

#### 1.1 Design Criteria

Load factors (n) for different categories:
- Utility category: +4.4g to -1.76g
- Aerobatic category: +6.0g to -3.0g
- Transport category: +2.5g to -1.0g
- UAV (small): +4.0g to -2.0g

Ultimate load = 1.5 × Limit load (typical safety factor)

#### 1.2 V-n Diagram

Key speeds:
- V_S: Stall speed at 1g
- V_A: Maneuvering speed = V_S × √n_max
- V_C: Cruise speed
- V_D: Dive speed
- V_NE: Never exceed speed

Gust load factor:
n_gust = 1 + (K_g × U_de × V × a) / (498 × (W/S))

Where:
- K_g = Gust alleviation factor
- U_de = Derived gust velocity (ft/s)
- a = Lift curve slope (per radian)

### 2. Wing Structural Analysis

#### 2.1 Wing Bending

Bending moment distribution (elliptical lift):
M(y) = (L/π) × √(1 - (2y/b)²) × (b/2 - y)

Maximum bending moment (at root):
M_root = (L × b) / (3π) ≈ 0.106 × L × b

For rectangular lift distribution:
M_root = L × b / 8 = 0.125 × L × b

#### 2.2 Spar Design

Required spar cap area:
A_cap = M_max / (σ_allow × h_spar)

Shear web thickness:
t_web = V_max / (τ_allow × h_spar)

Spar weight estimation:
W_spar ≈ 2 × ρ_material × A_cap × b/2

#### 2.3 Skin Stress

Skin buckling stress:
σ_cr = k × E × (t/b)²

Where:
- k = Buckling coefficient (depends on edge conditions)
- t = Skin thickness
- b = Stiffener spacing

Minimum gauge thickness:
t_min = 0.4 mm (composites), 0.8 mm (aluminum)

### 3. Fuselage Structural Analysis

#### 3.1 Semi-Monocoque Design

Skin stress from bending:
σ = M × y / I

Shear flow in skin:
q = V × Q / I

Torsion shear flow:
q_t = T / (2 × A_enclosed)

#### 3.2 Pressure Loads (Pressurized Fuselage)

Hoop stress:
σ_h = Δp × R / t

Longitudinal stress:
σ_l = Δp × R / (2t)

Cabin pressure differential:
Δp = p_cabin - p_ambient

Typical values: Δp = 8-9 psi for commercial aircraft

#### 3.3 Landing Gear Loads

Vertical load factor at touchdown:
n_v = 1 + (V_v / V_sink_design)²

Typical sink rate design: 10 ft/s
Gear load factor: 2.0-3.0g

### 5. Weight Estimation

#### 5.1 Statistical Weight Methods

Wing weight (General Aviation):
W_wing/S = 0.036 × S^0.758 × AR^0.6 × (W_to/S)^0.042 × (100×t/c)^-0.3

Fuselage weight:
W_fuse = K × S_wet × (L/D_fuse)^0.5

Tail weight:
W_tail = 0.04 × W_wing

#### 5.2 Component Weight Fractions

Typical weight breakdown (GA aircraft):
| Component | % MTOW |
|-----------|--------|
| Wing | 8-12% |
| Fuselage | 8-12% |
| Tail | 2-3% |
| Landing gear | 4-6% |
| Powerplant | 12-18% |
| Fixed equipment | 8-12% |
| Fuel | 15-25% |
| Payload | 20-30% |

Empty weight fraction:
W_e/W_to = 0.55-0.65 (GA)
W_e/W_to = 0.45-0.55 (transport)

#### 5.3 Center of Gravity

CG location:
x_cg = Σ(W_i × x_i) / Σ(W_i)

CG range requirements:
- Forward limit: Elevator authority to flare
- Aft limit: Longitudinal stability margin

Typical CG range: 15-30% MAC

### 6. Fatigue and Damage Tolerance

#### 6.1 S-N Curves

Basquin's equation:
σ_a = σ_f' × (2N_f)^b

Where:
- σ_a = Stress amplitude
- N_f = Cycles to failure
- b = Fatigue exponent (-0.05 to -0.12)

Endurance limit:
- Steel: ~0.5 × σ_ultimate
- Aluminum: No true endurance limit

#### 6.2 Damage Tolerance

Crack growth rate (Paris law):
da/dN = C × (ΔK)^m

Where:
- ΔK = Stress intensity range
- C, m = Material constants

Inspection intervals:
t_insp = a_crit / (da/dN)_max

#### 6.3 Composite Damage

Damage types:
- Matrix cracking
- Delamination
- Fiber breakage

BVID (Barely Visible Impact Damage):
- Threshold: 0.5-1.0 mm dent depth
- Design strain limit with BVID: 4000-5000 με

### 7. Joining Methods

#### 7.1 Riveted Joints

Rivet shear strength:
P_shear = τ_allow × A_rivet × n_rivets

Bearing strength:
P_bearing = σ_bearing × d × t × n_rivets

Rivet spacing:
- Edge distance: 2d minimum
- Pitch: 4-6d typical
- Row spacing: 4-5d

#### 7.2 Bonded Joints

Adhesive shear stress:
τ = P / (w × L_overlap)

Minimum overlap length:
L_min = σ_adherend × t / τ_adhesive

Typical adhesive properties:
- Shear strength: 20-40 MPa
- Peel strength: 5-15 kN/m

#### 7.3 Composite Joints

Bolt bearing stress:
σ_b = P / (d × t)

Bearing allowable: 600-900 MPa (typical CFRP)

Bolt hole stress concentration:
K_t = 3.0 (open hole)
K_t = 1.5-2.0 (filled hole)
