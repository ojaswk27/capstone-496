# Atmospheric Properties and Models
## Reference Document for Aerospace Design Assistant

### 1. International Standard Atmosphere (ISA)

#### 1.1 Sea Level Conditions

Standard sea level values:
- Temperature: T_0 = 288.15 K (15°C)
- Pressure: p_0 = 101,325 Pa (1013.25 hPa)
- Density: ρ_0 = 1.225 kg/m³
- Speed of sound: a_0 = 340.3 m/s
- Dynamic viscosity: μ_0 = 1.789 × 10⁻⁵ Pa·s
- Kinematic viscosity: ν_0 = 1.461 × 10⁻⁵ m²/s

#### 1.2 Temperature Profile

Troposphere (0-11 km):
T = T_0 + L × h
L = -0.0065 K/m (lapse rate)

At 11 km (tropopause):
T = 216.65 K (-56.5°C)

Stratosphere (11-20 km):
T = 216.65 K (isothermal)

Stratosphere (20-32 km):
T = 216.65 + 0.001 × (h - 20000)

#### 1.3 Pressure and Density

Troposphere (0-11 km):
p/p_0 = (T/T_0)^(g/(R×L)) = (T/T_0)^5.256
ρ/ρ_0 = (T/T_0)^(g/(R×L) - 1) = (T/T_0)^4.256

Isothermal layer (11-20 km):
p = p_11 × exp(-g(h-11000)/(R×T))
ρ = ρ_11 × exp(-g(h-11000)/(R×T))

Where:
- g = 9.80665 m/s²
- R = 287.05 J/(kg·K)

#### 2.1 Quick Approximations

Density ratio (troposphere):
σ = ρ/ρ_0 ≈ (1 - h/44330)^4.256

Pressure ratio (troposphere):
δ = p/p_0 ≈ (1 - h/44330)^5.256

Temperature ratio:
θ = T/T_0 ≈ 1 - h/44330 (for h < 11 km)

### 3. Non-Standard Atmospheres

#### 3.1 Hot and Cold Day

ISA +15°C (hot day):
T = T_ISA + 15
ρ = ρ_ISA × T_ISA / T (at same pressure altitude)

ISA -15°C (cold day):
T = T_ISA - 15
ρ = ρ_ISA × T_ISA / T

#### 3.2 Density Altitude

Density altitude accounts for non-standard conditions:
h_density = h_pressure + 120 × (T_actual - T_ISA)

Aircraft performance degrades at higher density altitude.

#### 3.3 Pressure Altitude

Pressure altitude from altimeter:
h_pressure = 44330 × (1 - (p/p_0)^0.19)

Altimeter setting:
QNH = Sea level pressure (for altitude above sea level)
QFE = Field elevation pressure (for height above field)
Standard = 1013.25 hPa (for flight levels)

### 4. Air Properties

#### 4.1 Speed of Sound

Speed of sound in air:
a = √(γ × R × T) = 20.05 × √T

Where γ = 1.4 for air.

At sea level: a = 340.3 m/s = 661.5 knots

#### 4.2 Dynamic Viscosity

Sutherland's formula:
μ = μ_0 × (T/T_0)^1.5 × (T_0 + S)/(T + S)

Where S = 110.4 K for air.

Approximate:
μ ≈ 1.458 × 10⁻⁶ × T^1.5 / (T + 110.4)

#### 4.3 Kinematic Viscosity

Kinematic viscosity:
ν = μ/ρ

At altitude, ν increases significantly:
ν_10km ≈ 3.5 × ν_0

### 5. Reynolds Number Effects

#### 5.1 Reynolds Number

Reynolds number:
Re = ρ × V × L / μ = V × L / ν

Characteristic lengths:
- Wing: Mean aerodynamic chord
- Fuselage: Length
- Propeller: Chord at 75% radius

#### 5.2 Critical Reynolds Numbers

Flow regime transitions:
- Laminar: Re < 5×10⁵ (flat plate)
- Transition: 5×10⁵ < Re < 10⁷
- Turbulent: Re > 10⁷

For airfoils:
- Laminar separation: Re < 10⁵
- Full turbulent: Re > 3×10⁶

#### 5.3 Scale Effects

Model to full-scale corrections:
- Match Reynolds number for accurate results
- Low Re models have higher C_D0
- Transition location varies with Re

### 6. Mach Number Effects

#### 6.1 Mach Number

Mach number:
M = V/a = V/√(γ×R×T)

Flow regimes:
- Subsonic: M < 0.8
- Transonic: 0.8 < M < 1.2
- Supersonic: 1.2 < M < 5
- Hypersonic: M > 5

#### 6.2 Compressibility Corrections

Prandtl-Glauert correction (subsonic):
C_p = C_p_incomp / √(1 - M²)

Critical Mach number:
M_crit: Where local flow first reaches M = 1

Drag rise Mach number:
M_dd: Where C_D increases by 0.002

#### 6.3 Transonic Effects

Wave drag appears above M_crit.
Total drag coefficient:
C_D = C_D0 + C_Di + C_D_wave

### 7. Wind and Turbulence

#### 7.1 Wind Profiles

Power law profile:
V(h) = V_ref × (h/h_ref)^α

Where α depends on terrain:
- Open water: α = 0.10
- Open terrain: α = 0.14
- Suburban: α = 0.22
- Urban: α = 0.33

#### 7.2 Gust Models

Discrete gust (1-cosine):
U = U_max/2 × (1 - cos(πs/H))

Where:
- s = Distance into gust
- H = Gust gradient distance (12.5c to 107c)

Continuous turbulence (Dryden/von Karman spectra):
Characterized by intensity σ and scale length L.

Light turbulence: σ = 1-2 m/s
Moderate: σ = 3-5 m/s
Severe: σ = 6-10 m/s

### 8. Practical Applications

#### 8.1 True vs Indicated Airspeed

True airspeed:
TAS = IAS × √(ρ_0/ρ) = IAS/√σ

Equivalent airspeed:
EAS = TAS × √σ = IAS (approximately)

Calibrated airspeed:
CAS = IAS + Position error correction

#### 8.2 Engine Performance

Power at altitude (normally aspirated):
P_alt = P_SL × σ^n

Where n ≈ 0.7-0.9.

Turbo/supercharged:
P_alt = P_SL up to critical altitude

#### 8.3 Takeoff and Landing

Takeoff distance varies:
d_TO ∝ 1/σ (longer in hot/high conditions)

Rate of climb varies:
ROC ∝ σ^0.5 to σ^0.7
