# Aircraft Weight Estimation Methods
## Reference Document for Aerospace Design Assistant

### 1. Weight Estimation Overview

#### 1.1 Design Process Weight Evolution

Conceptual design: ±20% accuracy
Preliminary design: ±10% accuracy
Detail design: ±5% accuracy
As-built: Actual weights

#### 1.2 Weight Breakdown Structure

Level 1 - Major groups:
- Structures
- Propulsion
- Equipment/Systems
- Useful load

Level 2 - Subgroups (structures):
- Wing
- Fuselage
- Empennage
- Landing gear

### 2. Statistical Weight Estimation

#### 2.1 Empty Weight Fraction

Historical correlation:
W_e/W_0 = A × W_0^C

Where A and C depend on aircraft type:

| Type | A | C |
|------|---|---|
| Sailplane | 0.91 | -0.05 |
| Powered sailplane | 1.02 | -0.05 |
| Homebuilt (metal) | 1.19 | -0.09 |
| Homebuilt (composite) | 1.15 | -0.09 |
| GA single | 2.36 | -0.18 |
| GA twin | 1.51 | -0.10 |
| Agricultural | 0.74 | -0.03 |
| Turboprop | 0.96 | -0.05 |
| Jet transport | 1.02 | -0.06 |

#### 2.2 Raymer's Method (Fixed Wing)

Wing weight:
W_wing = 0.036 × S^0.758 × AR^0.6 × (q×W_0/S)^0.006 × λ^0.04 × (100t/c)^-0.3 × (n_z×W_0)^0.49

Fuselage weight:
W_fuse = 0.052 × S_fuse^1.086 × (n_z×W_0)^0.177 × L_t^-0.051 × (L/D)^-0.072 × q^0.241

Horizontal tail:
W_ht = 0.016 × (n_z×W_0)^0.414 × q^0.168 × S_ht^0.896 × (100t/c)^-0.12 × AR_ht^0.043 × λ_ht^-0.02

Vertical tail:
W_vt = 0.073 × (n_z×W_0)^0.376 × q^0.122 × S_vt^0.873 × (100t/c)^-0.49 × AR_vt^0.357 × λ_vt^0.039

Landing gear:
W_lg = 0.095 × (n_l×W_l)^0.768 × (L_m/12)^0.409

Where:
- S = Wing area (ft²)
- AR = Aspect ratio
- q = Dynamic pressure (lb/ft²)
- λ = Taper ratio
- t/c = Thickness ratio
- n_z = Ultimate load factor
- n_l = Landing load factor
- L_t = Tail moment arm

### 3. Component Weight Methods

#### 3.1 Wing Weight

For general aviation:
W_wing/S = 2.5 + 0.8 × AR + 0.00125 × (W_0 × n_ult) / S

For high performance:
W_wing = K × S × b × √(n_ult × W_0 / S) × (1 + 2×λ) / (3 + 3×λ)

Material factor K:
- Aluminum: K = 0.0015
- Composite: K = 0.0012
- Wood: K = 0.0013

#### 3.2 Fuselage Weight

Cylindrical fuselage:
W_fuse = K × (L × D²) × √(n_ult)

Where:
- L = Length (m)
- D = Diameter (m)
- K ≈ 15-25 (depends on construction)

Rectangular fuselage:
W_fuse = K × (L × perimeter) × √(n_ult)

#### 3.3 Empennage Weight

Horizontal tail:
W_ht = 0.04 × S_ht × (AR_ht)^0.6 × √(n_ult × W_0)

Vertical tail:
W_vt = 0.03 × S_vt × (AR_vt)^0.5 × √(n_ult × W_0)

#### 3.4 Landing Gear Weight

Retractable gear:
W_lg = 0.043 × W_0^1.0 (for W_0 < 5000 lb)
W_lg = 0.040 × W_0^1.0 (for W_0 > 5000 lb)

Fixed gear:
W_lg = 0.033 × W_0^1.0

### 4. Powerplant Weight

#### 4.1 Reciprocating Engines

Specific weight:
- Air-cooled: 1.0-1.5 lb/hp
- Liquid-cooled: 1.3-2.0 lb/hp

Engine + accessories:
W_eng_installed = 1.3 × W_eng_dry

#### 4.2 Turboprop Engines

Specific weight: 0.3-0.5 lb/shp

Total powerplant:
W_pp = 1.4 × W_eng_dry

#### 4.3 Turbofan Engines

Specific weight: 0.2-0.4 lb/lbf thrust

Installed weight includes:
- Nacelle: 10-15% of engine
- Accessories: 5-10% of engine

#### 4.4 Electric Propulsion

Motor specific weight: 2-5 kW/kg (current tech)
ESC: 10-20 g per 10A capacity
Wiring: 5-10% of motor weight

Battery weight:
W_battery = E_required / (specific_energy × depth_of_discharge)

### 5. Equipment and Systems

#### 5.1 Avionics

Basic VFR: 20-40 lb
IFR equipped: 40-80 lb
Full glass cockpit: 60-120 lb
Commercial avionics: 200-500 lb

#### 5.2 Flight Controls

Manual controls: 0.5-1% of W_0
Power-boosted: 1-2% of W_0
Fly-by-wire: 1.5-3% of W_0

#### 5.3 Electrical System

Battery and generator: 0.5-1% of W_0
Wiring and equipment: 0.5-1.5% of W_0

#### 5.4 Hydraulics and Pneumatics

Light aircraft: 0.3-0.5% of W_0
Transport: 0.5-1% of W_0

#### 5.5 Furnishings

Pilot station: 20-40 lb
Each passenger: 10-30 lb
Cargo provisions: variable

### 6. Fuel Weight

#### 6.1 Fuel Required

Mission fuel:
W_fuel = W_0 × (1 - 1/exp(R × SFC / (V × L/D)))

Simplified (Breguet):
W_fuel = W_0 × (1 - exp(-R × SFC × D/(V × L)))

#### 6.2 Fuel System Weight

Fuel system:
W_fs = 0.02 × W_fuel (integral tanks)
W_fs = 0.04 × W_fuel (bladder tanks)

Auxiliary tanks add 5-10%.

### 7. UAV/Drone Weight Estimation

#### 7.1 Multirotor Components

Frame: 15-25% of AUW
Motors: 15-25% of AUW
Propellers: 2-5% of AUW
ESCs: 3-8% of AUW
Battery: 25-40% of AUW
Flight controller: 1-3% of AUW
Payload: 10-30% of AUW

#### 7.2 Fixed Wing UAV

Airframe: 30-40% of MTOW
Propulsion: 15-25% of MTOW
Battery/fuel: 20-35% of MTOW
Avionics: 5-10% of MTOW
Payload: 15-25% of MTOW

### 8. Weight Growth

#### 8.1 Weight Growth Factor

Design weight typically grows:
- From concept to first flight: +10-15%
- During development: +5-10%

Include margin:
W_design = W_estimated × 1.05 to 1.10

#### 8.2 Empty Weight Growth

ΔW_empty from modifications:
- Structural reinforcement: 5-10%
- Systems additions: 3-5%
- Producibility changes: 2-4%

### 9. Center of Gravity Estimation

#### 9.1 Component CG Locations

Wing: 40% MAC
Fuselage: 45% length
Horizontal tail: 40% MAC_ht
Vertical tail: 40% MAC_vt
Engine: Engine CG location
Landing gear: At attachment
Fuel: Tank centroid

#### 9.2 CG Calculation

x_cg = Σ(W_i × x_i) / Σ(W_i)

Similar for y_cg and z_cg.

#### 9.3 CG Travel

Empty to full fuel: Check CG travel
With/without payload: Check limits
Fuel burn: Progressive CG shift
