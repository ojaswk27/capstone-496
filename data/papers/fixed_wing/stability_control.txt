# Aircraft Stability and Control
## Reference Document for Aerospace Design Assistant

### 1. Static Stability Fundamentals

#### 1.1 Longitudinal Static Stability

For static stability: dCm/dCL < 0

Pitching moment equation:
Cm = Cm_0 + Cm_α × α + Cm_δe × δe + Cm_q × (q×c̄)/(2V)

Where:
- Cm_0 = Zero-lift pitching moment
- Cm_α = Pitching moment slope
- Cm_δe = Elevator effectiveness
- Cm_q = Pitch damping

Stability condition:
Cm_α < 0

#### 1.2 Neutral Point

Stick-fixed neutral point:
x_np/c̄ = x_ac_w/c̄ - Cm_α_w/CL_α_w + η × (S_h/S) × (l_h/c̄) × CL_α_h/CL_α × (1 - dε/dα)

Where:
- x_ac_w = Wing aerodynamic center (~0.25c)
- η = Tail efficiency factor (0.9-1.0)
- dε/dα = Downwash gradient (typically 0.3-0.5)

#### 1.3 Static Margin

Static margin:
SM = (x_np - x_cg) / c̄

Recommended values:
- Conventional aircraft: SM = 5-15%
- Aerobatic: SM = 0-10%
- Trainer: SM = 10-20%
- Fighter (fly-by-wire): SM = -15 to 0%

#### 1.4 CG Limits

Forward CG limit:
- Elevator authority to flare
- Nose-wheel lift-off capability
- Typically 15-25% MAC

Aft CG limit:
- Minimum static margin
- Dynamic stability requirements
- Typically 30-40% MAC

### 2. Lateral-Directional Stability

#### 2.1 Directional Stability

Weathercock stability:
Cn_β > 0 (positive for stability)

Yawing moment coefficient:
Cn = Cn_β × β + Cn_δr × δr + Cn_r × (r×b)/(2V)

Vertical tail contribution:
Cn_β_v = η_v × (S_v/S) × (l_v/b) × CL_α_v

#### 2.2 Lateral Stability (Dihedral Effect)

Roll due to sideslip:
Cl_β < 0 (negative for stability)

Contributors:
- Wing dihedral: Cl_β = -CL × Γ/57.3 (per degree dihedral)
- Wing sweep: Cl_β = -CL × tan(Λ) × 2π × (geometric factors)
- Wing position: High wing adds effective dihedral

Typical total: Cl_β = -0.05 to -0.15 per radian

#### 2.3 Dutch Roll Mode

Dutch roll is coupled yaw-roll oscillation.

Approximate frequency:
ω_dr ≈ √(Cn_β × (q×S×b)/(I_z))

Approximate damping ratio:
ζ_dr ≈ (Cn_r + Cl_β × I_z/(I_x × tan(β)))/(2 × ω_dr)

Requirements:
- ω_dr > 0.4 rad/s
- ζ_dr > 0.08
- ζ_dr × ω_dr > 0.15 rad/s

#### 2.4 Spiral Mode

Spiral stability criterion:
Cl_β × Cn_r - Cl_r × Cn_β > 0

If unstable (common), time to double amplitude should be > 20 seconds.

#### 2.5 Roll Mode

Roll mode time constant:
τ_r = -I_x / (q × S × b² × Cl_p / (4V))

Typical values: τ_r = 0.5-2.0 seconds

Roll rate response:
p_ss = Cl_δa × δa / Cl_p

### 3. Control Surface Design

#### 3.1 Elevator Sizing

Elevator area ratio:
S_e/S_h = 0.25-0.40 typical

Elevator effectiveness:
τ_e = dCL/dδ / (dCL/dα)

Typical τ_e = 0.4-0.6

Trim requirement:
δe_trim = -(Cm_0 + Cm_α × α_trim) / Cm_δe

Check maximum deflection < ±25°

#### 3.2 Aileron Sizing

Aileron span:
- Inboard: 0.6-0.7 × semispan
- Outboard: 0.9-0.95 × semispan

Aileron chord ratio:
c_a/c = 0.20-0.30

Roll rate requirement:
p × b / (2V) > 0.07 (for Class I aircraft)

#### 3.3 Rudder Sizing

Rudder area ratio:
S_r/S_v = 0.25-0.40

Critical sizing conditions:
- One engine inoperative (multi-engine)
- Crosswind landing
- Spin recovery

Minimum control speed:
V_mc = √(2 × T_yaw / (ρ × S × b × Cn_δr_max))

### 4. Dynamic Stability

#### 4.1 Longitudinal Modes

Short period mode:
ω_sp ≈ √(-(Z_α × M_q)/u_0 - M_α)
ζ_sp ≈ -(M_q + M_α̇ + Z_α/u_0)/(2 × ω_sp)

Requirements:
- ω_sp: 1-10 rad/s typical
- ζ_sp: 0.35-1.30 (Category A flight)

Phugoid mode:
ω_ph ≈ √2 × g/V
ζ_ph ≈ 1/(√2 × L/D)

Phugoid period: T = π × V / g ≈ 30-60 seconds

#### 4.2 Lateral-Directional Modes

Roll mode:
τ_r = -1/L_p

Dutch roll:
ω_dr ≈ √(N_β)
ζ_dr ≈ -N_r/(2 × ω_dr)

Spiral mode:
τ_s = (L_β × N_r - L_r × N_β)/(L_β × N_p)

### 5. Handling Qualities

#### 5.1 Cooper-Harper Rating

1-3: Satisfactory
4-6: Acceptable with deficiencies
7-9: Controllable with difficulty
10: Uncontrollable

#### 5.2 Control Harmony

Aileron/elevator force ratio: 1:2 to 1:4
Rudder/aileron force ratio: 1:1 to 2:1

Stick force gradients:
- Elevator: 1-5 lb/g
- Aileron: 0.5-3 lb/deg/s roll rate

#### 5.3 Stall Characteristics

Acceptable stall:
- Adequate warning (buffet, stick shake)
- Gentle break
- Wings level or slight roll
- Easy recovery

Unacceptable:
- Abrupt pitch break
- Uncontrollable roll
- Spin entry
- Deep stall

### 6. Tail Sizing

#### 6.1 Horizontal Tail Volume

Tail volume coefficient:
V_H = (S_h × l_h) / (S × c̄)

Typical values:
- GA aircraft: V_H = 0.5-0.7
- Transport: V_H = 0.8-1.2
- Fighter: V_H = 0.4-0.6

#### 6.2 Vertical Tail Volume

Tail volume coefficient:
V_V = (S_v × l_v) / (S × b)

Typical values:
- Single engine: V_V = 0.02-0.04
- Multi-engine: V_V = 0.06-0.10
- Fighter: V_V = 0.06-0.10

#### 6.3 Control Surface Hinge Moments

Hinge moment coefficient:
Ch = Ch_0 + Ch_α × α + Ch_δ × δ

Stick force:
F = q × S × c × Ch × (c̄/c_stick) / G

Where G is the gearing ratio.

### 7. Configuration Effects

#### 7.1 Canard Configuration

Advantages:
- Stall-proof (canard stalls first)
- Lower trim drag possible
- Attractive appearance

Disadvantages:
- Canard downwash affects wing
- Difficult to achieve high CL_max
- Spin recovery challenging

#### 7.2 Tailless/Flying Wing

Advantages:
- Low drag
- Structural efficiency
- Stealth potential

Disadvantages:
- Limited CG range
- Weak pitch control at low speed
- Requires careful design

#### 7.3 T-Tail

Advantages:
- Tail out of wing wake
- Better spin recovery
- Aesthetics

Disadvantages:
- Deep stall susceptibility
- Heavier structure
- Aeroelastic issues
