# Multirotor Motor and Propeller Selection Guide
## Reference Document for Aerospace Design Assistant

### 1. Motor Specifications

#### 1.1 Key Motor Parameters

KV Rating:
- Definition: RPM per volt (unloaded)
- Low KV (100-500): Large props, high torque, efficient
- Medium KV (500-1000): General purpose
- High KV (1000-2500): Small props, agile, racing

Motor Size Nomenclature:
- XXYY format (e.g., 2212)
- XX = Stator diameter (mm)
- YY = Stator height (mm)

Power = Voltage × Current:
P = V × I (Watts)

Torque:
τ = 9.55 × P / RPM (N·m)

#### 1.2 Motor Categories

Micro Motors (12xx-14xx):
- Stator: 12-14mm diameter
- Applications: Micro quads, 3" props
- Typical KV: 3000-5000
- Power: 50-150W

Small Motors (18xx-22xx):
- Stator: 18-22mm diameter
- Applications: 4-6" props, FPV racing
- Typical KV: 1600-2600
- Power: 150-400W

Medium Motors (23xx-28xx):
- Stator: 23-28mm diameter
- Applications: 7-10" props, photography drones
- Typical KV: 700-1200
- Power: 300-800W

Large Motors (30xx-50xx):
- Stator: 30-50mm diameter
- Applications: 12-18" props, heavy lift
- Typical KV: 100-500
- Power: 500-2000W

Industrial Motors (U-series):
- U8, U13, U15 designations
- Applications: 20-32" props, professional
- Typical KV: 85-170
- Power: 1000-5000W

#### 1.3 Motor Efficiency Curves

Efficiency varies with load:
- Peak efficiency: 70-85% of max power
- Light load (<20%): 50-60% efficiency
- Heavy load (>90%): 60-75% efficiency

Efficiency factors:
- Magnet quality (N52 > N48 > N42)
- Winding resistance
- Air gap
- Bearing quality

### 2. Propeller Selection

#### 2.1 Propeller Notation

Format: DDDDxPPPP or DDxP
- DDDD = Diameter (inches or mm)
- PPPP = Pitch (inches or mm)

Examples:
- 10x4.5: 10" diameter, 4.5" pitch
- 1045: Same as above
- 9450: 9.4" diameter, 5.0" pitch (DJI format)

#### 2.2 Pitch Concepts

Geometric pitch:
- Theoretical advance per revolution
- Higher pitch = more speed, less static thrust

Effective pitch:
- Accounts for slip
- Typically 60-80% of geometric pitch

Pitch-to-diameter ratio:
- Low (0.3-0.5): Static thrust, hovering
- Medium (0.5-0.7): General purpose
- High (0.7-1.0): Speed, forward flight

#### 2.3 Blade Count

2-blade:
- Most efficient
- Higher tip speed acceptable
- Racing, efficiency-focused

3-blade:
- Balance of thrust and efficiency
- Smoother
- General purpose

4+ blade:
- More thrust per diameter
- Lower efficiency
- Reduced noise (sometimes)

#### 2.4 Propeller Materials

Plastic (ABS/Nylon):
- Cheapest
- Breaks on impact (safety)
- Lower efficiency

Carbon-reinforced plastic:
- Stiffer, less flex
- Better efficiency
- Still breaks on impact

Carbon fiber:
- Highest efficiency
- Very stiff
- Can cause injury

Wood:
- Large props
- Efficient
- Custom pitches

### 3. Matching Motors and Propellers

#### 3.1 Basic Matching Rules

Motor KV × Propeller size relationship:
- Low KV → Large props
- High KV → Small props

Approximate max prop for motor size:
| Motor Size | Max Prop (inches) |
|------------|-------------------|
| 1306 | 4" |
| 1806 | 5" |
| 2204-2206 | 5-6" |
| 2212 | 8-10" |
| 2814 | 10-12" |
| 3508 | 13-15" |
| 4114 | 15-18" |
| U8 | 27-29" |

#### 3.2 Tip Speed Limits

Maximum tip speed:
V_tip = π × D × RPM / 60

Limits:
- Efficiency drops above 0.6 Mach
- Noise increases significantly above 0.7 Mach
- Structural limits vary

Maximum RPM for given diameter:
RPM_max = 0.7 × 343 × 60 / (π × D)

For 10" prop: RPM_max = 14,100

#### 3.3 Current and Power Limits

Motor current limit:
I_max specified by manufacturer

Propeller should not exceed motor limits:
- Check thrust test data
- Verify current draw at max throttle
- Allow 20% margin for battery sag

### 4. Performance Calculations

#### 4.1 Static Thrust Estimation

Simplified static thrust:
T ≈ K × D³·⁵ × pitch × RPM² × 10⁻¹⁰

More accurate (from momentum theory):
T = C_T × ρ × n² × D⁴

Where:
- C_T = Thrust coefficient (from test data)
- n = Rotational speed (rev/s)
- D = Diameter (m)

#### 4.2 Power Required

Power for thrust:
P = C_P × ρ × n³ × D⁵

Figure of merit:
FM = T^1.5 / (P × √(2 × ρ × A))

Ideal vs actual:
P_actual = P_ideal / FM

#### 4.3 Efficiency

Overall propulsive efficiency:
η_prop = T × V / P_mech

Static efficiency (hover):
η_static = T / P_elec = FM × √(2 × ρ × A × T) / P_elec

Efficiency ranges:
- Small props (3-5"): 50-65%
- Medium props (6-10"): 60-75%
- Large props (12"+): 70-85%

### 5. Application Guidelines

#### 5.1 Racing Quad (5" class)

Motor recommendations:
- Size: 2205-2306
- KV: 2300-2600 (4S), 1700-1900 (6S)
- Power: 500-700W max

Propeller recommendations:
- Size: 5040-5051
- 3-blade common
- Aggressive pitch for speed

Target thrust-to-weight: 8-12:1

#### 5.2 Freestyle Quad (5" class)

Motor recommendations:
- Size: 2306-2407
- KV: 2000-2400 (4S), 1500-1750 (6S)
- Power: 600-900W max

Propeller recommendations:
- Size: 5043-5146
- 3-blade, moderate pitch

Target thrust-to-weight: 6-10:1

#### 5.3 Photography Drone (1-3 kg)

Motor recommendations:
- Size: 2212-2814
- KV: 700-1000 (4S), 400-600 (6S)
- Power: 200-400W per motor

Propeller recommendations:
- Size: 9-12"
- Low-moderate pitch (4.5-5.5")
- 2-blade for efficiency

Target thrust-to-weight: 2.0-2.5:1

#### 5.4 Heavy Lift (10-25 kg)

Motor recommendations:
- Size: U8, U13, or similar
- KV: 100-170
- Power: 1000-3000W per motor

Propeller recommendations:
- Size: 24-32"
- Carbon fiber
- Folding for transport

Target thrust-to-weight: 1.8-2.2:1

### 6. Motor Testing Data

#### 6.1 Interpreting Test Data

Key parameters from thrust stand:
- Thrust (g or kg)
- Current (A)
- Power (W)
- Efficiency (g/W)
- RPM

Best efficiency point:
- Usually 40-60% throttle
- Match hover throttle to this range
