# Multirotor Control Systems and Flight Dynamics
## Reference Document for Aerospace Design Assistant

### 1. Coordinate Systems and Notation

#### 1.1 Reference Frames

Earth-Fixed Frame (NED):
- X_e: North
- Y_e: East  
- Z_e: Down

Body-Fixed Frame:
- X_b: Forward
- Y_b: Right
- Z_b: Down

#### 1.2 Euler Angles

Rotation sequence (3-2-1):
- ψ (Yaw): Rotation about Z_e
- θ (Pitch): Rotation about Y'
- φ (Roll): Rotation about X''

Rotation matrix R_eb:
R = R_z(ψ) × R_y(θ) × R_x(φ)

Angular velocity transformation:
[p]   [1    0       -sin(θ)   ] [φ̇]
[q] = [0  cos(φ)   cos(θ)sin(φ)] [θ̇]
[r]   [0  -sin(φ)  cos(θ)cos(φ)] [ψ̇]

### 2. Quadcopter Equations of Motion

#### 2.1 Translational Dynamics

Newton's equations in Earth frame:
m × ẍ = (cos(ψ)sin(θ)cos(φ) + sin(ψ)sin(φ)) × F_z
m × ÿ = (sin(ψ)sin(θ)cos(φ) - cos(ψ)sin(φ)) × F_z
m × z̈ = -m×g + cos(θ)cos(φ) × F_z

Where F_z is total thrust (negative up in NED).

Simplified (small angles):
m × ẍ ≈ F_z × (θ×cos(ψ) + φ×sin(ψ))
m × ÿ ≈ F_z × (θ×sin(ψ) - φ×cos(ψ))
m × z̈ ≈ -m×g + F_z

#### 2.2 Rotational Dynamics

Euler equations:
I_x × ṗ = (I_y - I_z) × q × r + τ_x
I_y × q̇ = (I_z - I_x) × p × r + τ_y
I_z × ṙ = (I_x - I_y) × p × q + τ_z

For symmetric quadcopter (I_x = I_y):
I_x × ṗ = τ_x
I_y × q̇ = τ_y
I_z × ṙ = τ_z (approximately)

#### 2.3 Motor Mixing (X Configuration)

Control inputs:
- U_1: Total thrust
- U_2: Roll torque
- U_3: Pitch torque
- U_4: Yaw torque

Motor speeds (squared):
ω_1² = (U_1 + U_2 - U_3 + U_4) / (4 × k_f)  [Front-Right, CCW]
ω_2² = (U_1 - U_2 - U_3 - U_4) / (4 × k_f)  [Rear-Right, CW]
ω_3² = (U_1 - U_2 + U_3 + U_4) / (4 × k_f)  [Rear-Left, CCW]
ω_4² = (U_1 + U_2 + U_3 - U_4) / (4 × k_f)  [Front-Left, CW]

Inverse (forces to control):
U_1 = k_f × (ω_1² + ω_2² + ω_3² + ω_4²)
U_2 = k_f × L × (ω_1² - ω_2² - ω_3² + ω_4²)
U_3 = k_f × L × (-ω_1² - ω_2² + ω_3² + ω_4²)
U_4 = k_m × (ω_1² - ω_2² + ω_3² - ω_4²)

Where:
- k_f = Thrust coefficient
- k_m = Moment coefficient
- L = Arm length

### 3. PID Control

#### 3.1 Basic PID Structure

Control output:
u(t) = K_p × e(t) + K_i × ∫e(t)dt + K_d × de/dt

Discrete implementation:
u[k] = K_p × e[k] + K_i × Σe[i]×Δt + K_d × (e[k] - e[k-1])/Δt

#### 3.2 Cascaded PID Architecture

Typical quadcopter control loops:
1. Inner loop (fast): Rate control (p, q, r)
2. Middle loop: Attitude control (φ, θ, ψ)
3. Outer loop (slow): Position/velocity control (x, y, z)

Rate controller (example for roll):
τ_x = K_p_rate × (p_cmd - p) + K_d_rate × ṗ

Attitude controller:
p_cmd = K_p_att × (φ_cmd - φ) + K_d_att × φ̇

Position controller:
φ_cmd = K_p_pos × (x_cmd - x) + K_d_pos × ẋ

#### 3.3 Tuning Guidelines

Starting values (for typical 1-2 kg quadcopter):
| Loop | K_p | K_i | K_d |
|------|-----|-----|-----|
| Roll/Pitch rate | 0.1-0.5 | 0.01-0.1 | 0.001-0.01 |
| Yaw rate | 0.5-2.0 | 0.1-0.5 | 0.01-0.05 |
| Roll/Pitch angle | 3-8 | 0-0.5 | 0.1-0.5 |
| Yaw angle | 2-5 | 0-0.2 | 0-0.1 |
| Position | 0.5-2 | 0-0.1 | 0.2-1.0 |
| Altitude | 1-3 | 0.1-0.5 | 0.2-1.0 |

Tuning order:
1. Rate controllers (inner loop first)
2. Attitude controllers
3. Position/altitude controllers

### 4. State Estimation

#### 4.1 Sensor Fusion

Complementary filter (attitude):
θ_est = α × (θ_est + gyro × dt) + (1-α) × θ_accel

Where:
- α = τ / (τ + dt)
- τ = Filter time constant (0.1-1.0 s typical)

#### 4.2 Kalman Filter

State prediction:
x̂_k|k-1 = F × x̂_k-1|k-1 + B × u_k
P_k|k-1 = F × P_k-1|k-1 × F^T + Q

Measurement update:
K = P_k|k-1 × H^T × (H × P_k|k-1 × H^T + R)^-1
x̂_k|k = x̂_k|k-1 + K × (z_k - H × x̂_k|k-1)
P_k|k = (I - K × H) × P_k|k-1

#### 4.3 IMU Specifications

6-DOF IMU typical specs:
- Gyroscope range: ±2000 °/s
- Gyroscope noise: 0.01-0.1 °/s/√Hz
- Accelerometer range: ±16g
- Accelerometer noise: 0.1-1 mg/√Hz
- Update rate: 100-1000 Hz

9-DOF IMU adds:
- Magnetometer for yaw reference
- Susceptible to magnetic interference

### 5. Trajectory Generation

#### 5.1 Minimum Snap Trajectories

Position trajectory as polynomial:
p(t) = Σ c_i × t^i (typically 7th order)

Minimum snap objective:
minimize ∫(d⁴p/dt⁴)² dt

Constraints:
- Position waypoints
- Velocity at waypoints
- Acceleration at waypoints

#### 5.2 Waypoint Following

Simple waypoint navigation:
v_cmd = K × (p_waypoint - p_current)

With velocity limiting:
v_cmd = min(v_cmd, v_max) × v_cmd/|v_cmd|

Arrival detection:
|p_waypoint - p_current| < d_threshold

### 6. Flight Modes

#### 6.1 Stabilize/Attitude Mode
- Pilot controls attitude directly
- Zero stick = level flight
- Altitude not controlled

#### 6.2 Altitude Hold
- Throttle controls climb rate
- Barometer/sonar for altitude
- Attitude still pilot controlled

#### 6.3 Position Hold (Loiter)
- GPS required
- Maintains position and altitude
- Stick inputs = velocity commands

#### 6.4 Auto/Mission
- Follows pre-programmed waypoints
- Fully autonomous
- Failsafe to RTL on signal loss

#### 6.5 Return to Launch (RTL)
- Climbs to safe altitude
- Flies to home position
- Lands automatically

### 7. Safety Features

#### 7.1 Motor Failure Handling

Quadcopter with one motor out:
- Cannot maintain yaw control
- Can maintain position by spinning
- Controlled descent possible

Hexacopter/Octocopter:
- Single motor failure recoverable
- Reduced performance
- Automatic motor redistribution

#### 7.2 Geofencing

Typical parameters:
- Maximum altitude: 120 m (regulatory)
- Maximum distance: 500-2000 m
- Minimum distance from home: 10 m
- No-fly zone avoidance

#### 7.3 Low Battery Actions

Voltage thresholds:
- Warning: 3.5 V/cell
- Critical: 3.3 V/cell
- Emergency: 3.0 V/cell

Actions:
- Warning: Alert pilot
- Critical: RTL initiated
- Emergency: Immediate landing

### 8. Common Flight Controller Specifications

#### 8.1 Processing Requirements
- Loop rate: 250-1000 Hz
- Processor: 32-bit ARM (Cortex-M4/M7)
- Flash: 512 KB - 2 MB
- RAM: 128 KB - 512 KB

#### 8.2 I/O Requirements
- PWM outputs: 4-8 channels
- Serial ports: 3-6 UARTs
- I2C, SPI buses
- ADC for voltage/current sensing
