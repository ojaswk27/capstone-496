# Multirotor UAV Design Principles and Calculations
## Reference Document for Aerospace Design Assistant

### 1. Introduction to Multirotor Aircraft

Multirotor unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs), commonly known as drones, are rotary-wing aircraft that achieve lift through multiple rotors. The most common configurations include quadcopters (4 rotors), hexacopters (6 rotors), and octocopters (8 rotors). Unlike traditional helicopters, multirotors achieve control through differential thrust rather than cyclic pitch control.

### 2. Fundamental Physics of Multirotor Flight

#### 2.1 Thrust Generation (Momentum Theory)

The thrust generated by a rotor in hover can be calculated using momentum theory:

T = 2 * ρ * A * v_i²

Where:
- T = Thrust (N)
- ρ = Air density (kg/m³), approximately 1.225 kg/m³ at sea level
- A = Rotor disk area (m²) = π * R²
- v_i = Induced velocity (m/s)

The induced velocity in hover is:
v_i = √(T / (2 * ρ * A))

#### 2.2 Power Required for Hover

The ideal power required for hover (induced power) is:
P_ideal = T * v_i = T^(3/2) / √(2 * ρ * A)

Accounting for non-ideal effects, the actual power is:
P_actual = P_ideal / FM

Where FM is the Figure of Merit, typically 0.6-0.8 for small rotors.

#### 2.3 Disk Loading

Disk loading is a key parameter for comparing rotorcraft efficiency:
DL = T / A = W / A (N/m²)

Lower disk loading generally results in better hover efficiency. Typical values:
- Small drones: 50-150 N/m²
- Large multirotors: 150-300 N/m²
- Helicopters: 200-500 N/m²

### 3. Motor and Propeller Selection

#### 3.1 Motor KV Rating

The KV rating indicates RPM per volt:
RPM = KV * Voltage

For a motor with KV = 920 and 4S battery (14.8V):
RPM_max = 920 * 14.8 = 13,616 RPM

#### 3.2 Thrust-to-Weight Ratio

For stable hover, the thrust-to-weight ratio (TWR) should be:
- Minimum for hover: TWR ≥ 1.0
- Recommended for agile flight: TWR ≥ 2.0
- Racing/acrobatic: TWR ≥ 3.0

Total thrust = Number_of_motors * Thrust_per_motor
TWR = Total_thrust / (Total_weight * g)

#### 3.3 Propeller Selection Guidelines

Propeller dimensions are specified as Diameter x Pitch (in inches):
- 10x4.5: 10-inch diameter, 4.5-inch pitch
- Larger diameter = more thrust, less efficiency at high speed
- Higher pitch = more speed, less static thrust

Static thrust approximation:
T ≈ (D/10)³ * (P/D) * (RPM/1000)² * 0.0254 (Newtons)

### 4. Battery and Endurance Calculations

#### 4.1 Battery Energy

Battery energy in Watt-hours:
E = (Capacity_mAh / 1000) * Voltage

For a 5000mAh 4S (14.8V) battery:
E = 5.0 * 14.8 = 74 Wh

#### 4.2 Flight Time Estimation

Flight time (minutes) = (Battery_capacity_mAh * 60) / (Average_current_draw_mA)

Or using power:
Flight_time = (Battery_energy_Wh / Power_consumption_W) * 60

#### 4.3 Hover Power Estimation

Approximate hover power for the entire aircraft:
P_hover = W^1.5 / √(2 * ρ * A_total)

Where:
- W = Total weight (N)
- A_total = Total disk area of all rotors

### 5. Quadcopter Dynamics and Control

#### 5.1 Equations of Motion

The quadcopter has 6 degrees of freedom: x, y, z (position) and φ, θ, ψ (roll, pitch, yaw).

Translational dynamics:
m * ẍ = (sin(ψ)sin(φ) + cos(ψ)sin(θ)cos(φ)) * U₁
m * ÿ = (-cos(ψ)sin(φ) + sin(ψ)sin(θ)cos(φ)) * U₁
m * z̈ = -g + cos(θ)cos(φ) * U₁/m

Where U₁ is the total thrust.

Rotational dynamics:
I_x * φ̈ = (I_y - I_z) * θ̇ * ψ̇ + L * U₂
I_y * θ̈ = (I_z - I_x) * φ̇ * ψ̇ + L * U₃
I_z * ψ̈ = (I_x - I_y) * φ̇ * θ̇ + U₄

Where:
- U₂ = Roll torque
- U₃ = Pitch torque  
- U₄ = Yaw torque
- L = Arm length from center to motor

#### 5.2 Motor Mixing

For a standard X-configuration quadcopter:
Motor 1 (Front-Right): ω₁² = (U₁ + U₂ - U₃ + U₄) / (4 * k_f)
Motor 2 (Rear-Right): ω₂² = (U₁ - U₂ - U₃ - U₄) / (4 * k_f)
Motor 3 (Rear-Left): ω₃² = (U₁ - U₂ + U₃ + U₄) / (4 * k_f)
Motor 4 (Front-Left): ω₄² = (U₁ + U₂ + U₃ - U₄) / (4 * k_f)

### 6. Structural Design Considerations

#### 6.1 Frame Materials

Common materials and properties:
- Carbon fiber: Density 1.6 g/cm³, Tensile strength 3500 MPa, E = 230 GPa
- Aluminum 6061-T6: Density 2.7 g/cm³, Tensile strength 310 MPa, E = 69 GPa
- ABS plastic: Density 1.05 g/cm³, Tensile strength 40 MPa, E = 2.3 GPa

#### 6.2 Arm Bending Analysis

Maximum bending stress in a cantilever arm:
σ_max = (M * y) / I = (F * L * t/2) / I

For a hollow square tube:
I = (b_outer⁴ - b_inner⁴) / 12

#### 6.3 Vibration Considerations

Natural frequency of arm:
f_n = (1.875²/2π) * √(E*I / (ρ*A*L⁴))

The motor RPM should not excite the arm natural frequency.

### 7. Typical Design Parameters

#### 7.1 Small Camera Drone (DJI Phantom class)
- Total weight: 1.2-1.5 kg
- Motor: 2212, KV 920-1000
- Propeller: 9-10 inch
- Battery: 4S 5000-6000mAh
- Flight time: 20-28 minutes
- Disk loading: ~80 N/m²

#### 7.2 Heavy-Lift Octocopter
- Total weight: 15-25 kg
- Motor: U13, KV 100-130
- Propeller: 28-32 inch
- Battery: 12S 16000-32000mAh
- Flight time: 15-25 minutes
- Disk loading: ~180 N/m²

#### 7.3 Racing Drone
- Total weight: 0.4-0.7 kg
- Motor: 2205-2306, KV 2300-2600
- Propeller: 5 inch
- Battery: 4-6S 1300-1800mAh
- Flight time: 3-6 minutes
- Thrust-to-weight: 8-12:1
