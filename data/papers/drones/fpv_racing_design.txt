# FPV Racing Drone Design and Configuration
## Reference Document for Aerospace Design Assistant

### 1. Racing Drone Categories

#### 1.1 Class Definitions

Micro class (Sub-250g):
- All-up weight < 250g
- 2.5-3" propellers
- Exempt from many regulations
- Indoor/outdoor capable

5" Class (Standard):
- Propeller size: 5"
- Frame size: 200-230mm
- Weight: 400-700g typical
- Most popular racing class

6-7" Class (Long Range):
- Propeller size: 6-7"
- Frame size: 250-300mm
- Weight: 600-900g
- Longer flight times

Cinewhoop:
- Ducted propellers
- Prop guards standard
- 2.5-3.5" props
- Safe for proximity

#### 1.2 Competition Standards

MultiGP spec class:
- 5" prop maximum
- 4S battery
- 600g weight limit

FAI F3U class:
- International racing standard
- Specific technical requirements
- Timing gate specifications

### 2. Frame Design

#### 2.1 Frame Configurations

True-X:
- Equal arm angles (90°)
- Balanced flight characteristics
- Most common racing design

Stretched-X:
- Front arms further apart
- Better camera clearance
- Improved forward flight

Dead Cat:
- Rear arms closer together
- Props out of camera view
- Popular for freestyle

Hybrid-X:
- Compromise design
- Various arm angles
- Manufacturer specific

#### 2.2 Frame Materials

Carbon fiber:
- 3K or 1.5K weave
- Thickness: 3-5mm arms, 2-3mm plates
- Excellent strength-to-weight
- Standard for racing

Carbon-aluminum hybrid:
- Aluminum standoffs
- Carbon plates
- Good vibration damping

3D printed:
- TPU for flex parts
- Quick replacement
- Heavier than carbon

#### 2.3 Stack Mounting

Standard stack:
- 30.5 × 30.5 mm spacing
- 20 × 20 mm (mini stack)
- M3 mounting holes

Component height:
- FC: 2-3 mm
- ESC: 3-4 mm
- VTX: varies
- Total stack: 15-25 mm

### 3. Motor Selection

#### 3.1 Motor Sizing

Common sizes for 5":
- 2205 (budget)
- 2206 (standard)
- 2306 (popular)
- 2207 (high power)
- 2407 (maximum)

First two digits: Stator diameter (mm)
Last two digits: Stator height (mm)

#### 3.2 KV Rating

4S battery (14.8V nominal):
- Racing: 2300-2600 KV
- Freestyle: 1900-2300 KV
- Cinematic: 1700-2000 KV

6S battery (22.2V nominal):
- Racing: 1700-1900 KV
- Freestyle: 1400-1700 KV
- Efficiency: 1200-1500 KV

Rule of thumb:
KV_6S ≈ KV_4S × 0.67

#### 3.3 Performance Metrics

Thrust-to-weight ratio:
- Cruising: 3-4:1
- Freestyle: 6-8:1
- Racing: 10-12:1

Efficiency target:
- Racing: 5-8 g/W
- Cruising: 8-12 g/W

### 4. ESC Configuration

#### 4.1 ESC Specifications

Current rating:
- 5" racing: 30-45A per ESC
- Continuous vs burst
- Include margin for peak

Voltage rating:
- 4S: Up to 17V
- 6S: Up to 26V
- Match battery voltage

Firmware:
- BLHeli_S (legacy)
- BLHeli_32 (current standard)
- AM32 (open source)

#### 4.2 4-in-1 ESC

Advantages:
- Compact
- Less wiring
- Integrated current sensor

Specifications:
- Individual ESC rating
- Total current capability
- BEC voltage/current

#### 4.3 ESC Settings

Motor timing: 22-26° typical
PWM frequency: 24-48 kHz
Motor direction: Props in or out
Demag compensation: Low/medium/high

### 5. Flight Controller

#### 5.1 Processor Options

F4 (STM32F405):
- 168 MHz
- Adequate for racing
- Budget option

F7 (STM32F722/745):
- 216 MHz
- Better filtering
- More features

H7 (STM32H743):
- 480 MHz
- Dual core option
- Maximum performance

#### 5.2 Gyro Specifications

IMU chips:
- BMI270 (common, good)
- ICM42688P (excellent)
- MPU6000 (legacy)

Sample rate:
- 8 kHz typical
- 32 kHz (high-end)

Filtering:
- Hardware low-pass
- Software notch filters
- Dynamic filtering

#### 5.3 Firmware Options

Betaflight:
- Most popular
- Racing optimized
- Active development

iNav:
- Navigation focused
- GPS features
- Autonomous flight

KISS/FETtec:
- Premium performance
- Closed ecosystem
- Race proven

### 6. FPV System

#### 6.1 Camera Specifications

Sensor size:
- 1/3" (standard)
- 1/2.5" (larger, better low light)
- Micro cased vs full size

Resolution:
- 800 TVL minimum
- 1200+ TVL (standard)
- 4K (digital systems)

FOV:
- 120-170° typical
- Wider = more situational awareness
- Narrower = better detail

Latency:
- Analog: <10 ms (glass-to-glass)
- Digital: 20-40 ms typical

#### 6.2 Video Transmitter (VTX)

Power levels:
- 25 mW: Short range, low interference
- 200 mW: Standard racing
- 400-600 mW: Long range
- 1000+ mW: Extreme range (legal issues)

Channels:
- 5.8 GHz: 40 channels common
- Raceband (R): 8 channels, good separation

Smart audio/IRC Tramp:
- OSD channel/power control
- Pit mode support

#### 6.3 Digital FPV Systems

DJI FPV:
- Excellent quality
- Higher latency (28-40 ms)
- Expensive

Walksnail/Avatar:
- Improving quality
- Lower latency
- More affordable

HDZero:
- Near-analog latency
- Lower resolution
- Racing focused

### 7. Battery Selection

#### 7.1 Cell Configuration

4S (14.8V):
- Standard racing
- Good power-to-weight
- Wide component support

6S (22.2V):
- Higher efficiency
- Lower current draw
- Becoming standard

#### 7.2 Capacity

Racing (5"):
- 4S: 1300-1500 mAh
- 6S: 1100-1300 mAh

Freestyle (5"):
- 4S: 1500-1800 mAh
- 6S: 1300-1550 mAh

Flight time:
- Racing: 2-4 minutes
- Freestyle: 4-7 minutes

#### 7.3 Discharge Rating

C-rating requirements:
- Racing: 75-100C+ burst
- Cruising: 45-75C adequate

Current calculation:
I_max = C_rating × Capacity_Ah

Example: 100C × 1.3 Ah = 130A burst

### 8. Propeller Selection

#### 8.1 Propeller Nomenclature

Format: DDPP×B or DDxPPxB
- DD: Diameter (inches)
- PP: Pitch (inches)
- B: Blade count

Examples:
- 5040×3: 5" diameter, 4" pitch, 3 blade
- 51499: 5.1" diameter, 4.99" pitch

#### 8.2 Pitch Effects

Low pitch (3.5-4"):
- More thrust
- Lower top speed
- Better control

High pitch (4.5-5"+):
- Higher top speed
- Less low-end thrust
- More amp draw

#### 8.3 Blade Count

2-blade:
- Most efficient
- Less thrust
- High-speed racing

3-blade:
- Balanced performance
- Standard racing choice
- Good all-around

4+ blade:
- Maximum thrust
- Lower efficiency
- Cinematic smoothness
