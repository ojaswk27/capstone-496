# UAV Power Systems and Battery Technology
## Reference Document for Aerospace Design Assistant

### 1. Lithium Polymer (LiPo) Battery Fundamentals

#### 1.1 Cell Specifications

Standard LiPo cell parameters:
- Nominal voltage: 3.7V per cell
- Fully charged voltage: 4.2V per cell
- Discharge cutoff voltage: 3.0-3.3V per cell
- Energy density: 150-250 Wh/kg
- Power density: 1000-3000 W/kg

Common configurations:
- 3S = 3 cells in series = 11.1V nominal (12.6V charged)
- 4S = 4 cells in series = 14.8V nominal (16.8V charged)
- 6S = 6 cells in series = 22.2V nominal (25.2V charged)
- 12S = 12 cells in series = 44.4V nominal (50.4V charged)

#### 1.2 C-Rating and Discharge

The C-rating indicates continuous discharge capability:
Maximum_current = Capacity_Ah * C_rating

For a 5000mAh 30C battery:
Max_current = 5.0 * 30 = 150A continuous

Burst ratings (typically 2x continuous):
Burst_current = 5.0 * 60 = 300A for short periods

#### 1.3 Internal Resistance Effects

Voltage sag under load:
V_actual = V_nominal - I * R_internal

For a battery with 5mΩ internal resistance at 100A:
Voltage_drop = 100 * 0.005 = 0.5V per cell

Power lost to internal resistance:
P_loss = I² * R_internal

### 2. Power Budget Analysis

#### 2.1 Hover Power Estimation

For a quadcopter, hover power can be estimated:
P_hover = (W^1.5) / (√(2 * ρ * A_total * η))

Where η is the overall propulsion efficiency (0.5-0.7 typical).

Example for 2kg drone with 10" props:
A_total = 4 * π * (0.127)² = 0.203 m²
P_hover = (2*9.81)^1.5 / √(2*1.225*0.203*0.6) = 230W

#### 2.2 Forward Flight Power

In forward flight, power decreases then increases:
P_forward = P_induced + P_parasite + P_profile

P_induced = W² / (2 * ρ * V * A) (decreases with speed)
P_parasite = 0.5 * ρ * V³ * Cd * S_ref (increases with speed³)
P_profile ≈ constant

Minimum power speed:
V_mp = √((4 * k * W²) / (3 * ρ² * Cd * S_ref * A))

#### 2.3 Climb Power

Additional power for climb:
P_climb = W * V_climb

Total power = P_hover + P_climb

### 3. Endurance and Range Calculations

#### 3.1 Hover Endurance

Endurance_hover = (E_battery * η_battery) / P_hover

Where η_battery accounts for usable capacity (typically 0.8).

For 74Wh battery, 230W hover power:
Endurance = (74 * 0.8) / 230 = 0.257 hours = 15.4 minutes

#### 3.2 Forward Flight Range

Range = V * Endurance
Range = (E_battery * η) / (P_total / V)

For minimum power speed, range is maximized:
Range_max = (E_battery * η * V_mp) / P_min

#### 3.3 Payload vs Endurance Tradeoff

Increased payload increases required thrust:
T_new = T_old + W_payload

Power scales approximately as:
P_new / P_old ≈ (W_new / W_old)^1.5

Endurance ratio:
E_new / E_old = (E_old / E_new) * (W_old / W_new)^1.5

### 4. Motor Efficiency and Matching

#### 4.1 Motor Efficiency Curve

Motor efficiency varies with operating point:
η_motor = P_mechanical / P_electrical
η_motor = (τ * ω) / (V * I)

Peak efficiency typically occurs at 70-85% of maximum power.

Efficiency vs throttle:
- 20% throttle: 50-60% efficiency
- 50% throttle: 75-85% efficiency  
- 80% throttle: 70-80% efficiency
- 100% throttle: 60-75% efficiency

#### 4.2 Motor-Propeller Matching

For optimal efficiency, match motor KV and propeller:
RPM_optimal = V_max * KV * throttle_hover

For hover at 50% throttle with 4S battery:
Target_RPM = 14.8 * 0.5 * KV = 7.4 * KV

Propeller tip speed should stay below Mach 0.7:
Max_RPM = (0.7 * 343) / (π * D) * 60

For 10" prop: Max_RPM = 14,100 RPM

#### 4.3 Electronic Speed Controller (ESC) Sizing

ESC current rating should exceed maximum motor current by 20%:
ESC_rating > 1.2 * Motor_max_current

ESC voltage rating must match battery:
ESC_voltage ≥ Battery_cells * 4.2V

### 5. Thermal Management

#### 5.1 Motor Temperature

Power dissipated as heat:
P_heat = P_electrical * (1 - η_motor)

Temperature rise:
ΔT = P_heat * R_thermal

Maximum motor temperature typically 80-100°C.

#### 5.2 Battery Temperature

Optimal operating temperature: 20-40°C
Maximum safe temperature: 60°C

Discharge capacity vs temperature:
- 0°C: 80% of rated capacity
- 25°C: 100% of rated capacity
- 40°C: 95% of rated capacity

### 6. Design Guidelines and Best Practices

#### 6.1 Power System Redundancy

For reliable operation:
- Dual batteries with isolation
- Multiple ESCs per motor (for octocopters)
- Voltage monitoring with failsafe

#### 6.2 Weight Distribution

Battery placement affects:
- Center of gravity (should be at rotor plane height)
- Moment of inertia (lower is more responsive)
- Crash survivability (protected location preferred)

#### 6.3 Wire Sizing

Wire gauge based on current:
- 14 AWG: 15A continuous
- 12 AWG: 20A continuous
- 10 AWG: 30A continuous
- 8 AWG: 50A continuous

Voltage drop in wires:
V_drop = I * R_wire * Length

### 7. Sample Power Budgets

#### 7.1 Photography Drone (1.5kg AUW)
| Component | Power (W) |
|-----------|-----------|
| 4x Motors (hover) | 200 |
| Flight Controller | 5 |
| GPS Module | 1 |
| Camera/Gimbal | 15 |
| Video TX | 3 |
| Receiver | 0.5 |
| LEDs | 2 |
| **Total Hover** | **226.5** |

With 4S 5000mAh (74Wh):
Flight time = 74 * 0.8 / 226.5 = 15.8 min

Flight time = 977 * 0.8 / 3293 = 14.2 min
