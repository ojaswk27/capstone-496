# CubeSat Design Standards and Guidelines
## Reference Document for Aerospace Design Assistant

### 1. CubeSat Form Factors

#### 1.1 Standard Unit Sizes

1U CubeSat:
- Dimensions: 100 × 100 × 113.5 mm
- Mass limit: 1.33 kg
- Volume: ~1 liter

2U CubeSat:
- Dimensions: 100 × 100 × 227 mm
- Mass limit: 2.66 kg
- Volume: ~2 liters

3U CubeSat:
- Dimensions: 100 × 100 × 340.5 mm
- Mass limit: 4.0 kg
- Volume: ~3 liters

6U CubeSat:
- Dimensions: 100 × 226.3 × 340.5 mm
- Mass limit: 12 kg
- Volume: ~6 liters

12U CubeSat:
- Dimensions: 226.3 × 226.3 × 340.5 mm
- Mass limit: 24 kg
- Volume: ~12 liters

#### 1.2 Dimensional Tolerances

Rail dimensions:
- Width: 8.5 mm ± 0.1 mm
- Minimum length: 85% of CubeSat length

Corner radius: 1 mm minimum

Separation springs:
- Location: ±0.5 mm from nominal
- Contact area: 4 rails

### 2. Deployer Interface Requirements

#### 2.1 P-POD Compatibility

Rail requirements:
- Hard anodized aluminum
- Surface roughness < 1.6 μm
- No burrs or sharp edges

Protrusions:
- Maximum beyond rails: 6.5 mm
- No protrusions in deployment direction

Access ports:
- RBF (Remove Before Flight) pins
- Separation switches
- Charging ports
- Umbilical connections

#### 2.2 Deployment Systems

Spring separation:
- Velocity: 0.5-2.0 m/s typical
- Angular rate limit: <10°/s

Deployment switches:
- Dual redundant
- Activate power on deployment
- Timer delay (30 min typical)

### 3. Structural Requirements

#### 3.1 Material Selection

Primary structure:
- Aluminum 6061-T6 or 7075-T6
- Avoid dissimilar metals (galvanic)
- Anodize for corrosion resistance

Fasteners:
- Stainless steel preferred
- Locking features required
- Torque specifications

Secondary structure:
- FR-4 PCB material
- Carbon fiber (CFRP)
- 3D printed polymers (some applications)

#### 3.2 Structural Loads

Launch loads (typical):
- Axial: ±10g
- Lateral: ±6g
- Combined: RSS method

Testing requirements:
- Random vibration: 14.1 grms typical
- Sine sweep: 0.5-100 Hz
- Shock: 200g at 1000 Hz

Safety factors:
- Yield: 1.25 (qual), 1.1 (flight)
- Ultimate: 1.4 (qual), 1.25 (flight)

#### 3.3 Venting

Depressurization rate:
- Maximum: 25 kPa/minute
- Vent area: Calculate from volume

Vent hole sizing:
A_vent = V × (dp/dt) / (C × P × √(T/M))

Where:
- V = Internal volume
- dp/dt = Pressure rate
- C = Discharge coefficient
- P, T = Pressure, temperature
- M = Molecular weight

### 4. Electrical Requirements

#### 4.1 Power System

Battery requirements:
- Li-ion or Li-poly preferred
- Protection circuit required
- Three inhibits to launch
- Capacity for mission

Solar panel sizing:
P_avg = η × S × A × F_eclipse × F_degradation

Where:
- η = Cell efficiency (20-30%)
- S = Solar constant (1361 W/m²)
- A = Cell area
- F_eclipse = Eclipse factor
- F_degradation = End of life factor

#### 4.2 Inhibits

Three inhibit requirement:
1. Remove Before Flight (RBF) pin
2. Deployment switch
3. Software timer (30 min)

All inhibits must be independent.

#### 4.3 RF Transmission

Pre-deployment:
- No RF transmission allowed
- Timer enforced (30 min minimum)

Frequency coordination:
- Amateur bands (UHF/VHF): ITU coordination
- S-band and above: License required
- IARU coordination for amateur

### 5. Thermal Design

#### 5.1 Thermal Environment

External temperatures:
- Sun-facing: +100°C
- Eclipse: -100°C
- Variation each orbit

Internal requirements:
- Electronics: -20°C to +60°C operating
- Batteries: 0°C to +40°C optimal

#### 5.2 Thermal Control Methods

Passive:
- Surface coatings (α/ε selection)
- MLI blankets
- Thermal straps
- Conduction paths

Active:
- Heaters (critical components)
- Thermostatic or software control
- Power budget consideration

#### 5.3 Analysis Requirements

Thermal model:
- Node-based or FEM
- Orbit average and extremes
- Eclipse worst case

Temperature margins:
- Operating: 5°C margin minimum
- Survival: 10°C margin minimum

### 6. Attitude Control

#### 6.1 Basic Stabilization

Passive methods:
- Gravity gradient (boom deployment)
- Magnetic hysteresis damping
- Permanent magnets

Performance:
- Gravity gradient: ±5-10° accuracy
- Magnetic: <45° pointing

#### 6.2 Active ADCS

Components:
- Magnetometers (3-axis)
- Sun sensors
- Gyroscopes (MEMS)
- Star tracker (optional)

Actuators:
- Magnetorquers (3-axis)
- Reaction wheels (optional)

Performance:
- Magnetorquers only: ±5° accuracy
- With wheels: ±1° or better
- With star tracker: ±0.1° possible

### 7. Communications

#### 7.1 UHF/VHF (Amateur)

Frequencies:
- Uplink: 145-146 MHz (2m)
- Downlink: 435-438 MHz (70cm)

Data rates:
- 1200 bps (AX.25)
- 9600 bps (common)
- 19200+ bps (possible)

Power:
- 0.5-2 W typical
- Omnidirectional antennas

#### 7.2 S-Band

Frequencies:
- 2.0-2.3 GHz typical

Data rates:
- 100 kbps - 10 Mbps

Antennas:
- Patch arrays
- 6-10 dBi gain

#### 7.3 Link Budget Example

3U CubeSat to ground (UHF):
- Tx power: 1 W (30 dBm)
- Tx antenna: 0 dBi
- FSPL (400 km): 145 dB
- Rx antenna: 12 dBi (Yagi)
- Rx noise figure: 2 dB
- Required Eb/N0: 10 dB
- Achievable rate: ~9600 bps

### 8. On-Board Computer

#### 8.1 Computing Requirements

Processor:
- ARM Cortex-M series (simple)
- ARM Cortex-A series (complex)
- Radiation considerations

Memory:
- RAM: 128 KB - 512 MB
- Flash: 1 MB - 8 GB
- Radiation tolerance

#### 8.2 Software Architecture

Flight software:
- Real-time operating system
- Command/telemetry handling
- Mode management
- Fault protection

Data storage:
- Telemetry archiving
- Science data storage
- Downlink queuing

### 9. Testing Requirements

#### 9.1 Environmental Testing

Vibration:
- Random: 14.1 grms
- Duration: 1-3 minutes per axis

Thermal vacuum:
- Cycles: 4-8 cycles
- Temperature range: -30°C to +60°C
- Dwell time: 1-2 hours

#### 9.2 Functional Testing

Pre-vibration:
- Full functional test
- Baseline performance

Post-vibration:
- Repeat functional test
- Compare to baseline

Post-TVAC:
- Full functional test
- Verify all systems
