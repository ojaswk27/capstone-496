# Satellite Communications Systems
## Reference Document for Aerospace Design Assistant

### 1. Link Budget Fundamentals

#### 1.1 Basic Link Equation

Received power:
P_r = P_t + G_t + G_r - L_fs - L_a - L_other (dB)

Where:
- P_t = Transmit power (dBW or dBm)
- G_t = Transmit antenna gain (dBi)
- G_r = Receive antenna gain (dBi)
- L_fs = Free space path loss (dB)
- L_a = Atmospheric loss (dB)
- L_other = Other losses (dB)

#### 1.2 Free Space Path Loss

FSPL = 20 × log₁₀(4πd/λ) dB
FSPL = 20 × log₁₀(4πdf/c) dB
FSPL = 32.45 + 20 × log₁₀(f_MHz) + 20 × log₁₀(d_km) dB

Example (LEO at 400 km, 2 GHz):
FSPL = 32.45 + 20 × log₁₀(2000) + 20 × log₁₀(400)
FSPL = 32.45 + 66 + 52 = 150.5 dB

#### 1.3 EIRP and G/T

Effective Isotropic Radiated Power:
EIRP = P_t + G_t (dBW)

Figure of merit (receive side):
G/T = G_r - 10 × log₁₀(T_system) (dB/K)

Where T_system is system noise temperature.

### 2. Antenna Fundamentals

#### 2.1 Antenna Gain

Parabolic antenna gain:
G = η × (πD/λ)² = η × (πDf/c)²

In dB:
G_dBi = 10 × log₁₀(η × (πD/λ)²)

Aperture efficiency η typically 0.55-0.65.

#### 2.2 Beamwidth

Half-power beamwidth (parabolic):
θ_3dB ≈ 70λ/D (degrees)

For 0.5 m dish at 10 GHz (λ = 3 cm):
θ_3dB = 70 × 0.03/0.5 = 4.2°

#### 2.3 Antenna Types for Small Satellites

Dipole/monopole:
- Gain: 2-5 dBi
- Omnidirectional
- Simple, lightweight

Patch antenna:
- Gain: 5-9 dBi
- Directional
- Compact, planar

Helix antenna:
- Gain: 10-15 dBi
- Circular polarization
- Moderate size

Horn antenna:
- Gain: 10-25 dBi
- Wide bandwidth
- Feed for reflectors

### 3. Modulation and Coding

#### 3.1 Digital Modulation

Bit error rate (BER) depends on Eb/N0:

BPSK/QPSK:
BER ≈ 0.5 × erfc(√(Eb/N0))

For BER = 10⁻⁵:
- BPSK: Eb/N0 ≈ 9.6 dB
- QPSK: Eb/N0 ≈ 9.6 dB
- 8PSK: Eb/N0 ≈ 13 dB
- 16QAM: Eb/N0 ≈ 13.5 dB

#### 3.2 Spectral Efficiency

Bits per symbol:
- BPSK: 1 bit/symbol
- QPSK: 2 bits/symbol
- 8PSK: 3 bits/symbol
- 16QAM: 4 bits/symbol

Bandwidth efficiency:
η_B = R_b / B (bits/s/Hz)

Nyquist bandwidth:
B = R_s × (1 + α)

Where α is roll-off factor (0.2-0.5).

#### 3.3 Forward Error Correction

Coding gain (typical):
- Convolutional (r=1/2): 5-6 dB
- Turbo codes: 8-10 dB
- LDPC: 9-11 dB

Coding rate trade-off:
- Lower rate = more protection = less throughput
- Higher rate = less protection = more throughput

### 4. Noise and Interference

#### 4.1 System Noise Temperature

T_system = T_antenna + T_receiver

Receiver noise temperature:
T_r = (F - 1) × T_0

Where F is noise figure and T_0 = 290 K.

Typical values:
- Room temp LNA: T_r = 50-150 K
- Cooled LNA: T_r = 10-30 K
- Uncooled space: T_r = 100-300 K

#### 4.2 Noise Power

Noise power:
N = k × T × B (Watts)
N_dBW = -228.6 + 10 × log₁₀(T) + 10 × log₁₀(B)

Where k = 1.38 × 10⁻²³ J/K (Boltzmann).

#### 4.3 Signal-to-Noise Ratio

C/N = P_r - N (dB)
C/N = EIRP + G/T - L_fs - k - 10×log₁₀(B)

Eb/N0 = C/N - 10 × log₁₀(R_b/B)

### 5. Link Budget Example

#### 5.1 LEO Satellite Downlink

Parameters:
- Frequency: 2.2 GHz (S-band)
- Orbit: 400 km
- Slant range: 800 km (low elevation)
- Data rate: 1 Mbps
- Required BER: 10⁻⁵

Satellite:
- Tx power: 1 W (0 dBW)
- Antenna gain: 6 dBi (patch)
- EIRP: 6 dBW

Losses:
- FSPL (800 km, 2.2 GHz): 162 dB
- Atmospheric: 0.5 dB
- Polarization mismatch: 0.3 dB
- Pointing loss: 1 dB

Ground station:
- Antenna: 3 m dish, G = 35 dBi
- System temp: 150 K
- G/T: 13.2 dB/K

Link calculation:
- C/N0 = EIRP + G/T - FSPL - L_other + 228.6
- C/N0 = 6 + 13.2 - 162 - 1.8 + 228.6 = 84 dB-Hz

Required C/N0:
- Eb/N0 required: 9.6 dB (QPSK, uncoded)
- C/N0 required = Eb/N0 + 10×log₁₀(1e6) = 9.6 + 60 = 69.6 dB-Hz

Margin: 84 - 69.6 = 14.4 dB ✓

### 7. Small Satellite Communications

#### 7.1 CubeSat Comm Systems

UHF/VHF (amateur bands):
- 437 MHz typical
- 1-10 kbps
- Simple, proven
- Global coverage

S-band:
- 2.0-2.3 GHz
- 100 kbps - 10 Mbps
- Higher data rates
- Requires ground infrastructure

X-band:
- 8.0-8.4 GHz
- 1-100 Mbps
- Image downlink
- Larger antennas needed

#### 7.2 Typical Power Budgets

UHF transmitter: 1-5 W
S-band transmitter: 1-10 W
X-band transmitter: 2-20 W

DC power consumption:
P_DC = P_RF / η_PA

Where η_PA = 20-50% (class AB/C amplifiers).

#### 7.3 Data Volume Calculations

Contact time per pass: 5-10 minutes
Passes per day: 4-8 (typical ground station)
Total contact time: 30-60 min/day

Data volume:
V = R × t_contact

At 1 Mbps for 30 min:
V = 1e6 × 30 × 60 / 8 = 225 MB/day

### 8. Ground Segment

#### 8.1 Ground Station Components

Antenna system:
- Dish size: 1-10 m (LEO)
- Tracking mount
- Feed and LNA

RF equipment:
- Low noise amplifier (LNA)
- Downconverter
- Modem/demodulator

Backend:
- Data recording
- Processing
- Distribution

#### 8.2 Tracking Requirements

LEO satellites:
- Angular rate: up to 2°/s at zenith
- Tracking accuracy: 0.1-1°
- Autotrack or program track

GEO satellites:
- Nearly stationary
- Manual or step track
- Pointing accuracy: 0.1°
